Uncertainty-aware navigation for Mars rovers

Submitted to NeurIPS 2026, RIT

A rover that loses contact with Earth has to decide alone. I designed a four-stage perception and planning system that turns terrain uncertainty into a real-time hazard map. A MobileNetV3-Small classifier supplies the uncertainty through MC Dropout (30 stochastic passes), which is blended with camera and LiDAR sensor fusion. Navigation runs in three layers: Theta* for global paths, a Double DQN agent for reactive driving, and D* Lite overrides when perception conflicts.

  • PyTorch
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Sensor fusion
  • Uncertainty estimation

Characterizing Hot Jupiters with network analysis

Carnegie Science, under review. Presented at AGU24.

I built unsupervised network pipelines in R that connect exoplanets by cosine similarity across many properties and reveal groups nobody had defined by hand. To keep the findings honest, I applied Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate control to the metallicity-radius correlations. I also used Kruskal-Wallis tests to separate real physical differences from survey detection bias, and made the graphs interactive with visNetwork.

Predicting solar flares: standard ML vs. Transformers

RIT term project, December 2025

I benchmarked Random Forest, XGBoost, KNN and a custom Transformer-based CNN/LSTM (SolarFlareNet) on 120,000 training samples of NASA SDO/SHARP magnetogram time series. Every model reached operational-grade skill (TSS above 0.50), KNN hit an F1 of 0.883 on M-class flares, and all models ran inference in under 100 ms, fast enough for real-time use.
