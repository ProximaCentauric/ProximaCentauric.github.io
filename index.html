Uncertainty-aware navigation for Mars rovers

Submitted to NeurIPS 2026, RIT

A rover that loses contact with Earth must determine how to navigate hazardous terrain without human intervention. I designed a four-stage perception and planning system that turns terrain uncertainty into a real-time hazard map. A MobileNetV3-Small classifier supplies the uncertainty through MC Dropout (30 stochastic passes), which is blended with camera and LiDAR sensor fusion. Navigation runs in three layers: Theta* for global paths, a Double DQN agent for reactive driving, and D* Lite overrides when perception conflicts.

  • PyTorch
  • Reinforcement learning
  • Sensor fusion
  • Uncertainty estimation

Characterizing Hot Jupiters with network analysis

Carnegie Science, under review. Presented at AGU24.

I created unsupervised network pipelines in R that connect exoplanets by cosine similarity across many properties and reveal five exoplanet categories nobody had defined by hand via Louvain community detection. After then running statistical analyses between metallicity and the five main features used, I applied Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate control to the metallicity-radius correlations to detect false positives. I also used Kruskal-Wallis tests to separate real physical differences from survey detection bias, and made the graphs interactive with visNetwork.

Predicting solar flares: standard ML vs. Transformers

RIT term project, December 2025

To compare traditional and transformer deep learning frame works in their abilities to predict different classes of solar flares, I benchmarked Random Forest, XGBoost, KNN and a custom Transformer-based CNN/LSTM (SolarFlareNet) on 120,000 training samples of NASA SDO/SHARP magnetogram time series. Every model reached operational-grade skill (TSS above 0.50), KNN hit an F1 of 0.883 on M-class flares, and all models ran inference in under 100 ms, revealing the limitations of standard, static forecasting for SolarFlarenet and the time-series limitations of conventional supervised ML models.
